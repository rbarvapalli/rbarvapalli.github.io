AI Safeguards

Egnyte AI Safeguards solves the access problem by adding a dedicated governance layer to your AI. Unlike standard file permissions, it controls what AI is allowed to read, summarize, or cite in real-time and gives you a full audit trail of every AI interaction.

Secure & Govern

This is Egnyte’s smart content governance solution. It comprises three distinct services: Content Classification, Access Control, and Content Lifecycles.
